# New Component Plan

## Component Selected: Node-cron (Scheduled Tasks)

### **Purpose**
I will involve the use of the Node-cron which will help me in creating scheduled tasks that are done at certain times (e.g., daily or weekly).  
Here, it will look into the case of books that are in default status of borrowing and automatically change the status.  
It can also send notifications that contain emails to users later using Nodemailer.

---

### **Why I Chose Node-cron**
- Easy to set-up and compliments with Express APIs.
- Perfect when maintenance is required like data cleanup, reminders or analytics updates.
